# Bundled fonts

These pixel fonts were made with [BitFontMaker2](https://www.pentacom.jp/pentacom/bitfontmaker2/) and are listed as
**Public Domain** in its gallery
([public-domain listing](https://www.pentacom.jp/pentacom/bitfontmaker2/gallery/?order=popular&license=pd)). Each was
downloaded from its gallery page. The author is the BitFontMaker2 username shown in the gallery, which is also the
copyright name in the font file.

The gallery also has a different, earlier "TimesNewPixel" by pentacom (id 57). The one here is IshPassion's.

Each is one scene unit to a font pixel at its design size, and crisp at whole multiples of it: 16 for most of them,
and 6 for TinyPixie2, which was drawn 6 pixels to the em.
